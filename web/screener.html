Best setups — the six-pillar checklist across today's candidates

Every stock the app already has a reason to look at (Candidates, Leaders, your watchlist, your holdings) is scored on Trend, Support/Resistance, Volume, Price action, Patterns and Risk plan. SETUP = 5-6 ticks and nothing against it. WATCH = worth following, something unclear. NO SETUP = the trend or the risk plan is against it. Runs after each close.

Leaders — relative strength vs EGX30

Stocks beating the index over 1, 3 and 6 months, still near their 52-week high, above the 50-day average and liquid enough to exit. Computed after each close from Yahoo daily candles; Refresh recomputes now (about a minute).

Patterns — reversal, triangles, continuation, wedges, channels, candlesticks, price action

Every pattern carries its category, direction, kind (reversal / continuation / indecision / structure), a reliability and frequency tier from the classic references, and — as it accumulates — the app's own EGX hit rate from the Scorecard. Confirmed = the trigger line was broken (or the candle/event completed) recently; Forming = shape complete, not yet broken. A finder, not a judge: the break with volume is what matters.

Signal Scorecard — which scanners actually work

Every scanner hit is graded by what the stock did 5, 10 and 20 sessions later, against EGX30 over the same window. A scanner's beat rate becomes its weight in the Candidates ranking — only after 20 graded hits, because a 60% hit rate on 5 trades is noise. Hits accumulate one day at a time from the post-close job.
